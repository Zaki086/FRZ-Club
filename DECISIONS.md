# DECISIONS.md

Decisions taken where `plan.md` was silent or self-contradictory, using the §16 rule
(satisfy the PS literally → simplest option that keeps every §9 invariant → record it here → continue).

| # | Date | Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-10-03 | The build prompt asks for a commit after every phase, but `Orchestration.md` says the Boss never commits on `main`; workers commit their own folders. | Phases are built and tested in the Boss workspace. At milestones the finished, tested work is handed to the four worker branches as Git patches (one per owned folder, see `Branches.md`), committed with `phase-N` messages and pushed by each account. `main` is updated only by squash-merging the agent-branch PRs. | Keeps the user's repo workflow (one account per branch, no conflicts) and still produces per-phase history. |
| D-02 | 2026-10-03 | Prisma cannot model `tstzrange` or exclusion constraints. | `court_reservations` and `shifts` keep `start_at`/`end_at` columns in Prisma; a generated column `period tstzrange GENERATED ALWAYS AS (tstzrange(start_at, end_at, '[)')) STORED` carries the exclusion constraint (raw SQL migration 0002). | The DB guarantee (BK-1, ST-2) stays exactly as specified while Prisma keeps working with plain timestamps. |
| D-03 | 2026-10-03 | §5.18 is "the complete list" of rejection codes, but an API also needs "not found" and "not logged in". | Two transport-level codes were added: `NOT_FOUND` (404) and `UNAUTHENTICATED` (401). They are never used for business-rule rejections. | Returning `VALIDATION_FAILED` for a missing record would mislead the UI. |
| D-04 | 2026-10-03 | How can the 60-day seed produce historical `created_at` values without bypassing the services? | `created_at` defaults to `app_now()`, which reads a transaction-local `app.now` that `withTx` sets from the injectable clock. | Seed rows carry their historical timestamps while still going through the real services (§11.2). |
| D-05 | 2026-10-03 | Hard-delete prevention (§2, §9.10) needs a mechanism, not just a convention. | `BEFORE DELETE` triggers raise on every transactional and master-data table; `ledger_entries`, `audit_logs`, `stock_movements` also reject `UPDATE`; `bill_lines` only allows its void marker to change. `verify:integrity` checks the triggers are installed. | §9.10 becomes a database guarantee. |
| D-06 | 2026-10-03 | Which roles see the GST report and the ledger explorer? §3 lists "Expenses/payables" and "Payroll" but not GST. | GST report and ledger explorer: OWNER and ACCOUNTANT (finance). MANAGER sees the operations dashboard and invoices only. | §3 says the Manager dashboard excludes payroll and net profit; tax is a finance function. |
| D-07 | 2026-10-03 | GST rates for court services, goods, restaurant and memberships. | Seeded placeholders (`verified = false`): court 18 %, membership 18 %, goods 12 %, services 18 %, restaurant 5 %, delivery 18 %, alcohol 0 % (alcohol is outside GST; state excise is not modelled). Settings shows a "verify rates" warning until the Owner confirms. | §11.1 says the team must verify current rates; the system never claims they are correct. |
| D-08 | 2026-10-03 | MB-7 sets the old membership's end date to "yesterday", which is before its start date when the upgrade happens on the start day. | The `memberships_dates` CHECK allows `end_date = start_date − 1` only for status `CHANGED`; such a membership never counts as active. | Keeps MB-7's rule literally without breaking the date sanity check for normal memberships. |
| D-09 | 2026-10-03 | §3 gives members "renew, upgrade" but not "downgrade". | Members can renew (same or any lower/equal plan at renewal is a downgrade by definition) from the portal; an explicit "schedule downgrade" (MB-8) is a front-desk action. | Follows the §3 matrix literally. |
| D-10 | 2026-10-03 | Prisma fills `@default(now())` on the client, so the DB-side clock default (D-04) was being bypassed. | `created_at` uses `@default(dbgenerated("app_now()"))`; `app_now()` is created at the top of migration 0001. A test proves seeded rows carry their historical timestamps. | Reports that use `created_at` (new members, new leads) must follow the injected clock. |
| D-11 | 2026-10-03 | How do cancellations close a bill? | Bills get `closed_at`: a booking/order cancelled in time is refunded first, then closed (status REFUNDED or VOID, nothing due). A late cancellation leaves the bill open, so any unpaid balance stays due (BK-7). Status is always re-derived from lines + payments in one UPDATE. | One clear rule for "what is still owed" across modules. |
| D-12 | 2026-10-03 | An online payment can succeed after its order's hold expired (customer paid late at the gateway). | The payment is recorded as SUCCEEDED and the amount no longer owed is refunded immediately through the same gateway, in the same transaction. | Money is never kept for a cancelled order and never silently dropped; the ledger nets to zero. |
| D-13 | 2026-10-03 | BR-8 says a tab is SETTLED only when paid = total, "TAB_HAS_BALANCE otherwise". | A partial settle records the payments and leaves the tab OPEN (the plan's own test: "partial settle → tab stays OPEN"). `TAB_HAS_BALANCE` is raised when someone tries to close a tab, or close the bar day, with a balance. | Matches both the rule and its test. |
| D-14 | 2026-10-03 | Bar and shop staff need to attach a tab or sale to a member, but §3 does not give them member access. | New capability `members.lookup` (all staff): name/phone/code search and QR card lookup only. Member 360 still requires `members.view`. | R-24 and R-27 need the member to be identified; the profile stays restricted. |
| D-15 | 2026-10-03 | When does an upgrade (MB-7) take effect if it is paid later? | Credit is computed when the upgrade is created (shown in the quote); the old membership becomes CHANGED and the new one ACTIVE when the bill is paid (MB-3). | The member never loses their current plan for an unpaid upgrade. |
| D-16 | 2026-10-03 | Which tier decides the booking window and the alcohol rule? | Booking window: the primary player's tier on the session date. Alcohol: the payer's tier and age today. | "Tier on the date of service" (PR-3) applied consistently. |
| D-17 | 2026-10-03 | Trial bookings: which window, and does a cancelled trial count? | Trials follow the walk-in window (today or tomorrow) because "all BK rules apply"; a phone that belongs to a member can't take a trial; a cancelled trial frees the phone (partial unique index excludes CANCELLED). | Literal reading of CR-8. |
| D-18 | 2026-10-03 | GST report basis. | Cash basis for the period: each bill's collections (net of refunds) in the period are spread over its lines; CGST/SGST vs IGST from the invoice's place of supply (B2C = club state). Labelled "report support". | Ties the report to the same ledger as the dashboard. |
| D-19 | 2026-10-03 | Payroll edge cases. | Gross = monthly salary for every active employee who joined by month end (no proration for joiners); employees with ₹0 salary (the owner) are excluded; deduction = unpaid leave days × salary ÷ days in month, rounded half-up. | ST-6 literally; proration was not specified. |
| D-20 | 2026-10-03 | Order states for pay-at-pickup. | ONLINE orders start PENDING_PAYMENT (15-min hold) and become CONFIRMED when paid; PAY_AT_PICKUP orders are CONFIRMED at once with a 48-hour hold and must be paid before COLLECTED. | SH-5/SH-6 combined. |
| D-21 | 2026-10-03 | Returns (SH-10) with immutable bill lines. | The returned line is voided and re-issued for the kept quantity at the same unit price and discount; the difference is refunded; stock comes back with a RETURN movement. | Keeps "total = sum of non-void lines" and the ledger invariant. |
| D-22 | 2026-10-03 | Kitchen display scope. | Every bar item (food and drinks) goes on the kitchen ticket; the KDS is the single "what to prepare and for whom" screen. | R-26: "the kitchen sees what to prepare and for whom". |
| D-23 | 2026-10-03 | Ports. | App on 3200, Postgres on 5442 (the host already runs other services on 3000/3100/5432). | Avoids clashes; configurable in `.env`. |
| D-24 | 2026-10-03 | Seed realism vs determinism. | The seed uses a fixed random seed and simulates staff/members trying things; business-rule rejections (slot taken, daily limit, out of stock…) are counted and printed, never forced through. Court 1 is kept free this evening and the 1-unit racket is never sold, for the §12 demo. | §11.2: everything through the services. |
| D-25 | 2026-10-03 | E-24 waitlist (P2). | Not built. | Time went to R-items, invariants and tests (§13 cut order: P2 first). |
